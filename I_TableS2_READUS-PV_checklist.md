**Manuscript:** *Remifentanil and hyperalgesia reporting in two national pharmacovigilance databases: a head-to-head disproportionality study with prespecified controls*

**How this checklist was completed.** Item wording is reproduced from the READUS-PV checklist (manuscript body, Table 1, and abstract, Table 2, of the development and statement [1]). ## Notes on items that could not be addressed

1. **Case-by-case analysis and causality assessment (Part A items 7d and 10; Part B item 2e).** Not performed. The FDA case-level and drug-record-level files that would permit individual case review were not retrievable from our working environment, and a case-by-case appraisal built on report-level openFDA records alone would not be valid. The limitation is stated in §4.6.
2. **Protocol registration (Part A item 14d).** The analysis was not prospectively registered. It was prespecified in the sense that the term groups, the negative controls and the specificity probe were fixed before the results were examined, and that specification is documented in §2.3 and archived with the analysis code in the study repository.
3. **Drugs included within the databases (Part A item 5a).** FAERS and Canada Vigilance are national spontaneous reporting systems open to all marketed medicinal products; neither is restricted to a fixed drug list, so the "type/number of drugs included" cannot be quantified. What is specified instead is the coding system used for drug names and the exact substance definitions of the four cohorts analysed (§2.2).
